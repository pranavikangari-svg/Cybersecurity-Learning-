## 1. What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, devices, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft.

**In simple words:**

> Cybersecurity means protecting digital systems and information from attacks.

---

## 2. CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a fundamental concept in cybersecurity.

| Principle          | Meaning                     | Simple Example                                      |
| ------------------ | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔒 Confidentiality | Keep information secret     | Only authorized people can access student records   |
| 📝 Integrity       | Keep information correct    | Someone cannot change marks without permission      |
| 🟢 Availability    | Keep information accessible | Students can access the college website when needed |

### Easy Memory Trick

* **C = Secret**
* **I = Correct**
* **A = Accessible**

---

## 3. Threats and Vulnerabilities

### Threat

A threat is something that has the potential to cause harm.

**Example:** A hacker trying to steal information.

### Vulnerability

A vulnerability is a weakness that an attacker can exploit.

**Example:** A weak password.

### Attack

An attack is an actual attempt to exploit a vulnerability or cause harm.

**Example:** A hacker tries to guess a weak password.

### Risk

Risk is the possibility of harm or loss resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability.

### Simple Example

```text
Hacker → Threat
Weak Password → Vulnerability
Password Guessing → Attack
Account Compromise → Possible Risk/Impact
```

---

## 4. Common Cyber Attacks

### 🎣 Phishing

Phishing uses fake messages, emails, or websites to trick people into giving sensitive information.

**Example:** A fake bank message asking for your password.

### 🦠 Malware

Malware means malicious software designed to harm systems, steal information, spy, or perform unauthorized actions.

Examples:

* Virus
* Worm
* Trojan
* Spyware
* Ransomware

### 🔐 Ransomware

Ransomware typically encrypts or blocks access to files and demands payment.

### 🔑 Brute Force

A brute-force attack tries many possible password combinations to discover a password.

### 💥 DoS

Denial of Service attempts to make a service unavailable to legitimate users.

### 🌐 DDoS

Distributed Denial of Service is similar to DoS, but the attack traffic comes from multiple systems or devices.

### 🕵️ MITM

Man-in-the-Middle occurs when an attacker gets between two parties communicating with each other.

### 🧠 Social Engineering

Social engineering involves manipulating or deceiving people to obtain information or make them perform an action.

---

## 5. Basic Security Practices

* Use strong and unique passwords
* Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* Keep software updated
* Be careful with unexpected links and attachments
* Back up important files
* Do not share passwords or authentication codes
* Be aware of phishing and social engineering
